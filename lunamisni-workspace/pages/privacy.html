Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Lunamisni is designed to process supported files inside your browser. This prototype does not upload files to a Lunamisni server, and it does not include accounts or analytics. PDF processing libraries and OCR language data may be loaded from their listed public CDNs; your files are passed to those libraries locally by your browser.

Files and processing

Files you select are read by the page and processed in the browser. Temporary preview and download URLs exist only for the current browser session. Closing the page clears that session's in-memory work. Do not use this prototype on shared devices for confidential documents.

External services

Google Fonts and public CDNs may receive standard connection information when your browser loads those resources. The site owner should review their privacy terms and self-host approved assets before public release. Contact links and analytics must be configured by the site owner; none are connected in this prototype.

Security and limitations

Browser-side processing reduces file transfer but is not a security certification. Some advanced operations are unavailable or have specific output limitations. Review each tool's result before relying on it, especially for legal, archival, or sensitive documents.
